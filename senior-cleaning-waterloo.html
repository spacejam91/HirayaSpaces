What makes a senior clean different in Waterloo

Waterloo has a large and growing senior population — from the long-established homes in Westmount and Beechwood to the newer retirement-friendly condos along Erbsville and University Avenue West. Cleaning for seniors here means understanding the specific homes our clients live in, and the specific needs that come with age:

  • Older Waterloo homes — character properties with original hardwood, plaster walls, and heritage tile. We use gentler products and softer techniques to protect what's irreplaceable.
  • Condo seniors in Uptown — King Street condos, low-floor units near grocery and pharmacy. We work quietly and respect shared-hallway rules.
  • Hard water — Waterloo Region water is famously hard. We descale showers and taps as part of every clean so safety bars stay grippy and showers stay slip-free.
  • Allergens & dust sensitivity — older lungs are more vulnerable. We use HEPA-filter vacuums and fragrance-free products on request.
