What's actually included in a Waterloo deep clean

Everything in a regular clean, plus:

  • Calcium descaling — Waterloo Region water runs 200–300 mg/L of dissolved minerals. Showers, taps, and glass build limescale faster here than in most cities. We treat it with the right acid product on every deep clean.
  • Baseboards & door frames — hand-wiped, not vacuumed
  • Inside the oven — racks out, grease cut, no chemical smell left behind
  • Inside the fridge — shelves removed and wiped, drawers cleaned, expired items flagged (with your permission)
  • Detailed bathroom scrubbing — grout, behind the toilet, vent covers
  • Light fixtures & ceiling fan blades — the spots dust hides on
