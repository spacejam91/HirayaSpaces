What's included in a Guelph deep clean

Everything in a regular clean, plus:

  • Calcium descaling — Guelph water comes from the same limestone aquifer as Waterloo Region. Showers, taps, and glass build limescale quickly here. Treated with the right acid product on every deep clean.
  • Baseboards & door frames — hand-wiped, especially important for older homes near downtown and the university
  • Inside the oven — racks out, full degrease
  • Inside the fridge — shelves and drawers
  • Detailed bathroom scrubbing — grout, behind toilet, vent covers
  • Light fixtures & ceiling fan blades
  • Original woodwork care — gentler hand on limestone-home trim and century baseboards
