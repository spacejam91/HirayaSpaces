What Cambridge homes typically need

Cambridge has a lot of century-old homes in Galt and Preston — beautiful, but they accumulate dust and grime differently than newer construction. Original woodwork, baseboards, and pre-war fixtures all need a gentler hand than spray-and-wipe. We adapt.

Newer subdivisions on the edges of Hespeler and Blair are more like Waterloo's family-home stock — straightforward regular or deep cleans, kitchens and bathrooms being the priority.
