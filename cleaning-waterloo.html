What KW homes typically need

Waterloo Region's water is hard — 200–300 mg/L of dissolved minerals from the limestone aquifer. That means calcium scale on tile, chrome, and glass shower doors builds up faster here than in most cities. We carry product specifically for it and treat it as part of every deep clean.
