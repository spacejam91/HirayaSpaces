What's a regular clean?

Your home stays clean between visits. Most of our regular-cleaning clients book bi-weekly — every other week, same day, same cleaner where possible. You skip the weekend cleaning slog, your space stays photo-ready, and we handle the dust before it becomes a project.

Frequently asked

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Most regular-cleaning clients leave a lockbox code or hidden key — we ask how you'd like us to get in during the booking. You'll get a notification when we arrive and again when we're done.

Will it be the same cleaner every time?

Yes, whenever possible. Consistency matters — your cleaner learns the quirks of your space, what you care about, and what to skip.

What's not included?

Heavy items, chandeliers, mold remediation, exterior windows, and biohazards aren't part of a regular clean. For deeper one-time work (move-out, post-renovation), see our Deep Clean or Move-in/Move-out services.
