Why families choose us for elderly cleaning

Helping an aging parent stay safely in their home is one of the most meaningful things you can do. Cleaning shouldn't feel like an intrusion — it should feel like a kindness. Here's what we do differently for senior clients:

  • Same cleaner every visit. Predictability matters. Your parent gets to know Jewel, and Jewel learns their home, their preferences, and their stories.
  • Background-checked. Every cleaner is vetted before they enter the home. Peace of mind for the adult children booking on a parent's behalf.
  • Gentle, patient pace. No rushing. We work around naps, visitors, and the realities of mobility limits.
  • Careful with belongings. Photo frames, knick-knacks, mementos — everything stays where it lives. We clean around, not through.
  • Fragrance-free and allergy-aware products on request. Sensitive lungs and skin are common at this stage of life.
  • Family communication. Adult children can book, pay, and receive updates — we route everything to the family member who manages mom or dad's care.

What's included in a senior care clean

Everything in a standard residential clean, with senior-specific care woven in:

  • Kitchen: counters, sink, stovetop, microwave, fridge exterior, dishwasher exterior
  • Bathrooms: toilet, sink, mirror, tub/shower, floor — extra attention to grab bars and shower seats
  • Dusting all surfaces — including ceiling fans, fixtures, blinds
  • Vacuuming carpets and area rugs — gentle on slippery rugs and runners
  • Mopping hard floors — non-slip products available
  • Trash out, beds made (if you'd like)
  • Empty medication area / wipe down without disturbing pills
  • Wipe walkers, wheelchairs, mobility aids on request
