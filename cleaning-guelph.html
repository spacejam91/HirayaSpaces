What Guelph homes typically need

Guelph's housing stock leans older than Kitchener-Waterloo's, especially closer to downtown and the university. Limestone homes have their own quirks — original woodwork, deep window wells, century baseboards. We adapt to the home rather than rushing through a template.

South-end builds in Westminster Woods, Pine Ridge and the newer subdivisions are more like Waterloo's family-home stock — kitchen and bathroom focus, with regular maintenance dominating bookings.
